## Trading Journey

Darian's trading journey spans quantitative backtesting, algorithmic signal research, and systematic strategy development — all built on portable SQLite-backed data pipelines.

## Research Projects

- **[backtestgosqlite](/backtestgosqlite/)** — High-performance backtesting engine storing all tick data and P&L in SQLite
- **[backtest-sell-limit](/backtest-sell-limit/)** — Multi-scenario sell-limit order execution research
- **[backteststoxx](/backteststoxx/)** — Concurrent STOXX market data extraction from Gmail into SQLite
- **[crisis-winner](/crisis-winner/)** — Identifying equities resilient during the 2020 and 2022 market crises
- **[study-3-signals](/study-3-signals/)** — 3-signal momentum framework (BAR, SPY, BND) for SPXL prediction

## Philosophy

Every backtest result lives in a single portable SQLite file — no proprietary platforms, no lock-in. Query your own trade history with plain SQL.
