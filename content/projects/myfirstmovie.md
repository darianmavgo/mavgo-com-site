**Genre:** Sci-Fi / Mystery / Thriller  
**Running Time:** 110 Minutes  
**Budget:** $5M (Tier 2 Low Budget)  
**Status:** Pre-Production

## Logline
When a disgraced astrophysicist detects a pattern in cosmic background radiation that matches a childhood lullaby, she must decode the message before a rogue private military corporation silences the signal—and her—forever.

## Project Overview
This repository contains all production assets, scripts, schedules, and legal documentation for the feature film "Signal Fracture". It is organized to facilitate collaboration between the production office, creative heads, and post-production teams.

## Directory Structure
- **Development/**: Screenplays, treatments, character bios, and research.
- **Pre-Production/**: Budgets, schedules, casting, locations, and shot lists.
- **Production/**: Call sheets, daily production reports (DPR), and release forms.
- **Post-Production/**: Editing logs, VFX breakdowns, sound spotting sheets.
- **Marketing/**: Pitch decks, poster concepts, press releases.
- **Legal/**: Contracts, agreements, insurance, and chain of title.
- **Distribution/**: Film festival strategy, distributor lists, deliverables.
